# Verviq — College Outreach Kit (1-Day Bootcamp Pitch)

First target: the **TPO / Training & Placement Officer** (they own placement training). CC the placement cell coordinator. The Principal usually enters after the TPO shows interest.

---

## Subject line options (pick one)

1. `Interview-readiness bootcamp for your final-years — from a [Hometown] founder`
2. `A 1-day placement bootcamp for [College Name] — no lectures, promise`
3. `Your students know the answers. Let's help them say them.`

---

## The email

> **Dear [Dr./Mr./Ms. Last Name],**
>
> I'm [Your Name], founder of **Verviq** — and [a Computer Engineering (Cybersecurity) graduate of Karunya University], from [Your Hometown] itself.
>
> Before starting up, I spent a week on the other side of the interview table, conducting **around 500 candidate interviews**. The same thing kept happening: technically strong students losing opportunities — not because they didn't know the answer, but because they couldn't present it.
>
> Verviq fixes exactly that. We run highly interactive interview-readiness programs — mock interviews, rapid-fire HR rounds, group-discussion battles and timed speaking challenges — where students *practice* being interviewed instead of sitting through lectures about it.
>
> I'd like to conduct a **complimentary 1-day Placement Confidence Bootcamp** at [College Name] for your pre-final and final-year students:
>
> - **Morning** — communication & confidence games, the "HR Hot Seat" rapid-fire round
> - **Afternoon** — live mock interviews + a GD battle, with individual feedback for every participant
> - **Close** — a mini placement simulation, plus a skills summary report the college keeps
>
> There's **no fee** for this first bootcamp. I'm selecting a small group of founding campus partners for 2026, and I'd be glad for [College Name] to be one of them.
>
> Could we take **15 minutes this week** — a call or a campus visit — so I can show you a quick live demo of the challenges?
>
> With great communication comes great opportunities.
>
> Warm regards,
> **[Your Name]**
> Founder, Verviq
> 📞 [Phone] · ✉️ [Email]

## Sending tips

- **Send Tue–Thu, 9:30–11:30 AM** — TPOs live in meetings; morning mail gets read.
- **No attachments in mail #1** (spam filters + TPOs skim). Offer the one-pager/demo in the call.
- The **hometown + Karunya angle is your trust hook** — lead with it, it's why you're not a random vendor.
- "Free first bootcamp" is the wedge, not the business. After the bootcamp, you hand over the skills report and pitch the **4-week Placement Sprint** for the full batch.
- Ask for a photo + a 2-line note from the TPO after the bootcamp → that becomes your first real testimonial for the website.

## Bootcamp → client playbook

1. Free 1-day bootcamp (this email) →
2. Deliver + hand the college a per-student skills summary →
3. Pitch the 4-week Placement Sprint for the placement-season batch →
4. Collect testimonial + photos → first real social proof on the Verviq site.
